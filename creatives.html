Build Over Nights, Kiro x Quick Hackathon 2026 Branding Kit

AWS User Group Philippines · Marketing & Creatives Committee Co-Lead

We started by brainstorming two directions. Mine, “Precision Under Pressure,” took inspiration from Kiro and Quick: a clean palette, modern type, glass-inspired materials, and soft ambient lighting for a premium AI feel, instead of the usual cyberpunk hackathon look. Viv’s, “The Anatomy of Controlled Chaos,” captured the adrenaline of an all-night hackathon with heavy texture, bold type, and a red palette.

After feedback on keeping the premium direction, I combined the two because I liked Viv’s story. The final kit pairs the clean, glassy base with textured assets: low-opacity grain, giant faded text, glass, and gradient light, in a purple-to-pink palette on near black, set in SF UI Display.
